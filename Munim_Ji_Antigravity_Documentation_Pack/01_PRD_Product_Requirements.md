# Munim Ji — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11 October 2026 | **Status:** Build baseline  
**Product:** Munim Ji — Your Personal Business Manager  
**Domain:** MunimJee.com  
**Primary users:** Indian business owners, SME owners, multi-business owners, business managers

## 1. Product vision
Munim Ji is a business control and intelligence system that helps an owner understand the health of one or more businesses from structured daily manager reports. Managers submit operational and financial data; the platform computes metrics; Munim Ji explains what the data suggests and recommends actions in plain language; the owner decides what to do.

**Core principle:** Managers provide the data. Munim Ji understands the data. The owner makes better decisions.

## 2. Problem statement
Business owners often rely on scattered spreadsheets, messaging apps, verbal updates, and delayed summaries. This makes it difficult to compare businesses, detect deteriorating performance, follow capital recovery, or ensure that daily reporting is happening consistently.

## 3. Product boundaries
### In scope for MVP
- Owner registration, login, profile and profile-photo upload/crop.
- Multiple business creation and configuration.
- Separate onboarding paths for **New** and **Existing** businesses.
- Investment and baseline capture appropriate to each path.
- Manager accounts, assignment, activation/deactivation, reassignment and password reset flow.
- Daily Business Health Reports.
- Reporting calendar with submitted / partial / missed states.
- Owner Master Control Room dashboard.
- Business detail with Overview, Performance, Financial Journey, Operations and History tabs.
- Derived business metrics, alerts, in-app notifications.
- Munim Ji verdict engine and text-first Ask Munim Ji.
- Learning/calibration period before confident AI verdicts.
- English/Hindi UI and AI/report output; numbers remain numeric and unchanged.
- Definition popups for business terms.
- ₹499 consultation request flow via WhatsApp, email or callback; record request in database and integrate Razorpay if payment is collected in-app.
- PDF exports with embedded permanent “Generated by Munim Ji – Confidential” watermark.
- Mobile-first navigation and responsive desktop experience.

### Explicitly out of scope
- POS, billing, barcode scanning, GST billing.
- SKU-level inventory management.
- Replacement for full accounting software.
- Voice AI at launch.
- Complex chart library or multiple chart types.
- Push notifications at launch.
- Historical CSV import at launch; this is a later roadmap item.

## 4. User roles and permissions
### Owner
- Can view and manage only businesses they own or are explicitly authorized to manage.
- Can invite/create managers and assign them to specific businesses.
- Can see owner dashboard, metrics, reports, alerts, AI verdicts and exports for authorized businesses.
- Can manage profile, language, notifications and consultation requests.

### Manager
- Can sign in and access only assigned business reporting workflows.
- Can submit and view permitted reports for assigned businesses, subject to product rules.
- Cannot access the owner's strategic dashboard, other businesses, investment details unless specifically needed for a form, AI owner conversations, or other managers' private data.

### Platform operator/support
- No default unrestricted access to identifiable customer business data.
- Prefer aggregate/anonymized analytics.
- Exceptional support access must be explicitly authorized, time-bounded where practical, least-privilege and audited.

### Munim Ji AI
- Receives only the minimum authorized, business-scoped metrics/context needed for a particular request.
- Must not access data by bypassing authorization, RLS or server-side scope checks.
- Must not invent missing metrics or present uncertain conclusions as facts.

## 5. Business creation flows
### New business
Capture business identity and complete starting point:
- Name, business type, industry, location, start date, ownership, outlets and objective.
- Initial investment, owner capital, additional investment, working capital, setup/equipment costs, deposit/security, initial inventory investment.
- Monthly fixed expenses, expected investment recovery period and break-even target/period.
- Validate currency, non-negative amounts, dates and required fields.
- Explain financial terms in English/Hindi.
- Recovery and break-even metrics start from the documented baseline.

### Existing business
Use a short Business Snapshot, not a forced historical backfill:
- Approximate operating age.
- Current profitability (self-reported).
- Typical monthly revenue and expense ranges.
- Whether original investment has already been recovered.
- Known ongoing problem area.
- Optional baseline data if the owner knows it.
If investment is marked already recovered, hide/retire recovery-progress metrics for that business. Do not show a misleading zero or stale metric.

## 6. Functional requirements
**FR-01 Authentication:** Secure owner and manager sign-in, sign-out, session handling, password recovery and account state handling.

**FR-02 Business management:** Owners can create, edit, archive/deactivate and switch between authorized businesses. Business start type is immutable after creation unless an explicit migration flow is designed.

**FR-03 Manager management:** Owner can create/invite a manager, activate/deactivate, reassign, and initiate reset. Assignment changes must take effect immediately at the database authorization layer.

**FR-04 Daily report:** Capture revenue, expenses, cash/bank collection, outstanding amounts, major expenses, operational issues and notes. Define whether fields are daily totals, cash-basis collections or accrual figures; show help text to reduce inconsistent reporting.

**FR-05 Reporting calendar:** Each business/date has a deterministic state: submitted, partial or missed. Define submission cutoff/time zone and how edited/reopened reports affect status.

**FR-06 Dashboard:** Show total invested capital where meaningful, current revenue/profit, health status, recovery progress where applicable, break-even progress where applicable, critical businesses, recent reports, recommendations and notifications.

**FR-07 Chart:** One vertical bar chart with modes: Revenue & Profit Trend, Profit by Business, Capital Allocation. Keep a consistent business color mapping. Provide accessible labels and empty states.

**FR-08 Business detail:** Five tabs—Overview, Performance, Financial Journey, Operations, History—with consistent date range and metric definitions.

**FR-09 Verdict engine:** Return structured status (Growing / Stable-Watch / Critical, or Learning), reason, financial impact, recommended action, priority and confidence. Explain the evidence window and missing data.

**FR-10 Ask Munim Ji:** Text-first Q&A grounded only in authorized business data. Cite the metric/date range used in the answer where practical. Ask a clarifying question if the required metric is unavailable.

**FR-11 Learning period:** For approximately the first 2–4 weeks of real daily reports, show a clear calibration message rather than a confident verdict. Make the threshold configurable and define the exact minimum data-quality rule before production.

**FR-12 Alerts and notifications:** Every alert includes why it triggered, affected period, severity, recommended next action and a link to a real destination. Notification bell opens a popup with read/unread state.

**FR-13 Take Action:** Every recommendation routes to an appropriate real action: view report, investigate a metric, contact manager, or request a consultation. Do not show decorative dead-end CTAs.

**FR-14 Consultation:** Capture ₹499 consultation request, preferred contact route (WhatsApp/email/callback), contact details, consent, status, timestamps and payment reference if applicable. Do not claim payment succeeded until provider confirmation.

**FR-15 PDF reports:** All-business summary and single-business report. Embed “Generated by Munim Ji – Confidential” into the generated PDF itself. Include generation timestamp, selected period, metric definitions and appropriate disclaimers.

**FR-16 Localization:** English/Hindi toggle applies to UI, validation, empty states, notifications, AI outputs and report content. Never translate digits, currency amounts, dates in a way that changes meaning, or database values.

**FR-17 Realtime:** Subscribe only to authorized, relevant business IDs. Realtime updates must not leak cross-tenant data and should degrade gracefully if disconnected.

**FR-18 Definitions:** Business terms open short plain-language definitions in English and Hindi.

## 7. Key user journeys
1. Owner signs up → sets language/profile → creates business → chooses New or Existing → completes correct onboarding → lands on dashboard.
2. Owner creates manager → assigns manager to Business A → manager signs in → submits daily report → calendar and owner dashboard update.
3. Owner reviews a Critical alert → sees evidence and impact → opens relevant report/metric → takes action or requests consultation.
4. Owner asks Munim Ji a question → server verifies owner and business scope → retrieves approved metrics → LLM produces grounded answer → UI displays answer with evidence/time range.
5. Owner exports a PDF → selects all businesses or one business and period → server generates PDF with embedded watermark → file is authorized and downloadable.

## 8. Non-functional requirements
- Security: database-enforced tenant isolation using Supabase RLS; server-side validation; least privilege.
- Reliability: errors are understandable, retryable where safe, and logged without exposing secrets.
- Performance: dashboard reads precomputed metrics; avoid expensive aggregation on every page load.
- Accessibility: keyboard operation, readable contrast, form labels, clear focus states, chart text alternatives.
- Responsive: mobile-first, tablet and desktop layouts.
- Auditability: log manager assignment changes, access-sensitive actions, AI generation metadata and report generation.
- Maintainability: typed data contracts, migrations in version control, automated tests, staging separate from production.
- Privacy: data minimization, retention policy and documented deletion/export processes.

## 9. Success metrics
- Owner onboarding completion rate.
- Percentage of active businesses with reports submitted on schedule.
- Manager report completion rate.
- Time from report submission to dashboard update.
- Percentage of AI answers with valid supporting metrics.
- Alert-to-action rate.
- PDF generation success rate.
- Cross-tenant authorization tests passed (target: 100%).
- Support incidents caused by confusing metrics or false AI confidence.

## 10. MVP release gates
Do not onboard real managers until RLS and API isolation review passes.
Do not enable confident AI verdicts until daily reports flow, metric definitions are tested, and calibration rules are implemented.
Do not enable payment collection until payment verification and duplicate-webhook handling are tested.
Do not release PDF export until the watermark is embedded in the file and access controls are verified.
