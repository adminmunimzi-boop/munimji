Munim Ji — Complete Build & Handover Guide

Product: Munim Ji — Your Personal Business Manager Tagline: Your Personal Business Manager Category: Business Management + Business Intelligence + AI Guidance Primary market: Indian business owners, SMEs and multi-business owners Domain: MyMunimJee.com Backend: Supabase (Auth, Postgres, RLS, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions) Build tool: Lovable (frontend, wired directly to Supabase)

This document is written to be handed to a developer as-is. It covers what to build, why, in what order, with what tools, and how to know each phase is actually done.

1. What Munim Ji Is

An owner running one or several businesses often has information scattered across managers, accountants, spreadsheets, messaging, and different outlets. Munim Ji becomes the owner's central business control and intelligence system: managers submit structured daily reports, the platform computes business health from that data, and an AI layer (Munim Ji) turns it into plain-language verdicts and a recommended next action.

Core philosophy: Managers provide the data. Munim Ji understands the data. The owner makes better decisions.

What it is NOT:

  • Not a POS — no billing, barcode, SKU-level inventory, or GST billing at this stage.
  • Not just a CRM — the manager side is a Business Health Reporting Tool for structured recurring data capture, not a sales pipeline tool.
  • Not a generic AI chatbot — it answers using the owner's own authorized business data, not generic business advice.

2. User Journeys

2.1 Business Owner Journey (end to end)

Account → Business → Investment/Snapshot → Operations → Reports → Analysis → Action → Growth
  1. Create account, set up profile (photo, name, email, phone, language preference).
  2. Create a business — and immediately answer New or Existing (see Section 4; this is the single most important fork in the entire product).
  3. If New: define the business's starting point — full investment and cost structure. If Existing: complete the short Business Snapshot instead.
  4. Create and assign a manager to the business.
  5. Manager starts daily reporting.
  6. Data accumulates in Supabase.
  7. Munim Ji analyses the business (once enough data exists — see the Learning Period in Section 4).
  8. Owner sees business health on the dashboard.
  9. Alerts and recommendations appear as conditions warrant.
  10. Owner takes action — views detail, resolves an issue, or books a consultation.
  11. Results are tracked over time: investment recovery, break-even progress, growth/expansion readiness.

2.2 Manager Journey

  1. Receives login credentials from the owner (manager accounts are owner-created, not self-service).
  2. Logs in — sees only their assigned business(es), never the owner's strategic dashboard.
  3. Submits the daily Business Health Report (see Section 5.2 for the exact fields).
  4. Can view their own reporting history/calendar.
  5. Has no access to investment figures, AI verdicts, or other businesses on the platform.

2.3 Multi-Business Concept

A single owner can run several businesses, each fully independent:

Owner → Business A / Business B / Business C → individual reporting + portfolio intelligence

Each business has its own managers, reports, investment information, performance history, alerts, and Munim Ji analysis. The owner additionally sees a combined portfolio view across all of them.

3. Scope

In scope (MVP)

  • Owner registration/login, profile (with photo upload/crop)
  • Multiple business creation, each with full setup (name, type, industry, location, start date, ownership, outlets, objective)
  • New vs Existing business branch at creation — see Section 4, this governs the rest of the setup flow
  • Investment & financial starting point per business: initial investment, owner capital, additional investment, working capital, setup/equipment costs, deposit/security, initial inventory investment, monthly fixed expenses, expected investment recovery period, break-even period — required for New businesses; optional/derived from the Snapshot for Existing ones
  • AI "learning period" state — see Section 4
  • Manager creation, login, and account management (activate/deactivate, reassign, reset password) — managers only access their assigned business and reporting functions
  • Daily Business Health Report submission (revenue, expenses, cash/bank collection, outstanding amounts, major expenses, operational issues, notes)
  • Manager reporting calendar (green = submitted, red = missed, yellow = partial)
  • Owner Dashboard ("Master Control Room"): total invested capital, current revenue/profit, business health, recovery progress, break-even progress, critical businesses, recent reports, Munim Ji recommendations, notifications
  • One vertical bar chart with a mode switch (Revenue & Profit Trend / Profit by Business / Capital Allocation) — not multiple chart types
  • Consistent per-business color coding across the dashboard
  • Business Detail page with five tabs: Overview, Performance, Financial Journey, Operations, History
  • Munim Ji AI: Ask Munim Ji (text first; voice architecture planned, not required at launch) + Verdict Engine (status: Growing / Stable-Watch / Critical; reason; financial impact; recommended action; priority; confidence)
  • Take Action system — every alert leads to a real next step, up to and including booking a consultation
  • ₹499 paid 1-on-1 consultation flow (WhatsApp / email / callback request), recorded in Supabase
  • In-app notification bell (popup, not a separate page)
  • Definition popups for business terms (English + Hindi)
  • English ↔ Hindi language toggle across the entire app (numbers never translate)
  • PDF reports (All Businesses Summary or Specific Business), with a permanent non-removable "Generated by Munim Ji – Confidential" watermark
  • Mobile-first design: bottom nav (Home / Businesses / Munim Ji / Alerts / Profile), swipeable business cards, floating Ask Munim Ji button

Explicitly out of scope (do not build)

  • Full POS, billing, barcode, or GST billing systems
  • Complex or SKU-level inventory management
  • Multiple chart types / overly complex graphing
  • Any generic accounting-software replacement

These may be reconsidered later, but only if they directly serve the long-term Munim Ji strategy — they should not creep into this build.

On the roadmap, not required for launch

  • CSV import of historical records for existing-business owners who already keep informal records (commonly in Excel) — a simple template for the last 6–12 months of monthly totals. The Business Snapshot (Section 4) covers launch-day needs for this segment; CSV import is a later refinement.
  • Voice interaction for Ask Munim Ji ("Hi Munim Ji" wake phrase) — architecture should allow for it, but browser/OS background-microphone restrictions mean it should not be promised as working at launch.
  • Browser/mobile push notifications — the in-app bell is sufficient for MVP; build notification architecture in a way that supports push later without rework.

4. Locked Decision: New vs Existing Business Onboarding

The problem: The product as originally specced assumes every business starts at zero — Day 1 investment entered fresh, recovery and break-even tracked from a known starting point. This works beautifully for a brand-new business and breaks for an owner who has run a business for years, since nobody will backfill years of daily history, and metrics like "investment recovery" may already be irrelevant to them.

The reframe: This is not a reason to treat existing businesses as a worse fit. The core pain Munim Ji solves — "I can't be in three places at once, and my managers tell me whatever they want" — is felt more by an established multi-outlet owner than by someone who just opened their first shop. The fix is not to fake historical data; it's to stop making investment-recovery the mandatory front door for a segment it doesn't apply to.

The solution — implemented as a fork at business creation:

  • New business: full Day 1 setup as specified in Section 3 — investment entered fresh, recovery/break-even tracked from a true zero.

  • Existing business: a short Business Snapshot (5–6 questions, not a data backfill) instead of reconstructing history:

    • Roughly how long has the business run?
    • Is it currently profitable? (self-reported)
    • Typical monthly revenue range
    • Typical monthly expense range
    • Has the original investment already been recovered? (yes/no)
    • Any known ongoing problem area?

    If investment is marked as already-recovered, that metric retires from the dashboard for this business — it is not shown as zero or stale, it simply doesn't display.

AI Learning Period (applies to every business, matters most for Existing ones): For roughly the first 2–4 weeks of real daily reports, Munim Ji shows an honest "still learning your business's rhythm" message instead of issuing a confident Growing/Stable-Watch/Critical verdict. A wrong early verdict off three days of data is worse than an honest "still calibrating" message — especially for a skeptical, experienced owner who already knows their business better than a three-day-old app.

Data model impact:

  • businesses gets a business_start_type field (new / existing).
  • New table: business_baseline_snapshots — one row per Existing business, holding the Snapshot answers.

5. Feature Detail by Area

5.1 Business Creation

Fields: business name, type/category, industry, location, start date, ownership type, number of outlets, business description, business objective — plus the New/Existing fork from Section 4.

5.2 Daily Business Health Report (manager-submitted)

  • Reporting date
  • Daily revenue/sales
  • Daily expenses
  • Cash collected
  • Bank/digital collection
  • Outstanding amount (where applicable)
  • Major expenses
  • Operational issues
  • Business notes
  • Manager observations

This is deliberately a five-minute daily summary, not a transaction-level POS log. Design note for the developer: keep revenue/expenses as fast numeric entry (under 30 seconds), and treat notes/observations as optional so the daily habit doesn't die from friction — a manager who finds this annoying will simply stop submitting.

5.3 Manager Reporting Calendar

Green = report submitted, Red = report missed, Yellow = partially completed. Manager can view their own history; owner can see whether managers are submitting consistently. This is the product's built-in accountability mechanism — it answers "is my manager actually consistent?" without the owner having to ask.

5.4 Manager Account Management

Owner creates manager profile, assigns to a business, sets password, can change/reset password, activate/deactivate, and reassign. A manager only ever accesses their assigned business/reporting permissions — never the owner's strategic portfolio dashboard.

5.5 Owner Dashboard ("Master Control Room")

Should immediately answer: What is happening across my businesses?

  • Total invested capital
  • Current revenue
  • Current profit
  • Business health
  • Investment recovery progress
  • Break-even progress
  • Critical businesses
  • Recent manager reports
  • Munim Ji recommendations
  • Important notifications

5.6 Dashboard Chart — Final Decision

Only one chart is displayed at a time. Type: vertical bar chart. A small filter/switch in the chart's corner changes the same container between three modes: Revenue & Profit Trend, Profit by Business (₹L/month), Capital Allocation. Must be readable on mobile, show exact values on hover/tap, use consistent business colors, and update when underlying data changes. Do not build multiple chart types.

5.7 Business-Specific Colors

Each business gets a consistent color, retained across the dashboard and all charts. Keep colors accessible and avoid visual overload as the number of businesses grows.

5.8 Business Detail Page

Opens when a business card is clicked. Five tabs:

  • Overview: current health, investment, revenue, profit, recovery progress, break-even, Munim Ji verdict
  • Performance: revenue/profit/expense trends, health and historical comparison
  • Financial Journey: initial investment, additional investment, capital used, recovery, remaining recovery, break-even progress
  • Operations: daily reports, manager performance, missing reports, operational notes
  • History: reports, alerts, important events, decisions/actions

5.9 Munim Ji AI

Ask Munim Ji: text conversation at launch (voice architecture planned, not required — see Section 3). Example questions it should handle: Why did my profit fall? Which business is performing best? Where am I losing money? Which business needs attention? How much investment have I recovered? Am I ready to expand? What should I do this week? Compare all my businesses.

Verdict Engine returns, per business:

  • Status: Growing / Stable-Watch / Critical-At Risk
  • Reason: what is causing the status
  • Financial Impact: potential financial implication
  • Recommended Action: what the owner should do
  • Priority: High / Medium / Low
  • Confidence: High / Medium / Low, informed partly by data completeness/quality

Remember the Learning Period requirement from Section 4 — this engine must have a defined "not enough data yet" state, not just a confident guess.

5.10 Take Action System

Every alert leads to a real next step:

  • Normal → view detailed business information
  • Warning → view problem/risk details
  • Critical → view detailed business analysis + recommended action + consultation option

5.11 Critical Business Consultation

CTA: Book 1-on-1 Business Consultancy — ₹499. Channels: WhatsApp, Email, Request a Call Back. The request records owner, business, contact information, reason, date/time, and relevant critical-condition context in Supabase. Tone should be helpful, not fear-based.

5.12 Notifications

The notification bell opens a slide-down/modal popup — never a separate page. Types: Munim Ji critical alert, manager submitted report, manager missed report, business health warning, mandatory action, consultation recommendation. Notifications are clickable and lead to the relevant business/action. Build the architecture so future browser/mobile push is a straightforward addition, not a rebuild.

5.13 Definition Popups

A small (i) info icon next to business terms that may not be immediately understood (Revenue, Profit, Break-even, Investment Recovery, Capital Allocation, Operating Expense, Cash Flow, Burn Rate, ROI if it appears in imported data). Tap/click shows a small 1–2 line, non-technical popup without navigation. Must support English and Hindi.

5.14 Language Toggle — Final

English ↔ Hindi toggle. All relevant content changes: dashboard text, buttons, labels, notifications, definitions, AI interface, reports, navigation. Numbers never change (e.g. "Monthly Profit: ₹2,50,000" stays as digits in both languages). Persist the language preference across sessions.

5.15 Profile System

Owner's profile picture shows top-right on the homepage/header; clicking opens the profile section: photo, full name, email, phone, account info, owner role, language preference, account settings, and photo upload/crop/adjust/preview/save/replace, with the saved image reflecting immediately in the header.

5.16 Navigation

Home always returns to the main dashboard while preserving login session, language, and relevant preferences.

5.17 PDF Reports

Owner can generate an All Businesses Summary Report or a Specific Business Report, using live data: business information, investment, performance, revenue, expenses, profit, recovery, break-even, trends, alerts, and Munim Ji insights/verdict. Every generated PDF must carry a non-removable watermark: "Generated by Munim Ji – Confidential," baked into the document itself, not a removable UI overlay. Report language follows the user's selected language; numbers stay unchanged. Cloud-based PDF generation is fine initially; architecture should allow swapping in a self-hosted generator later.

5.18 Mobile Experience

Not a compressed desktop view — designed intentionally for mobile: bottom navigation (Home / Businesses / Munim Ji / Alerts / Profile), large readable KPIs, the single vertical bar chart, swipeable business cards, easy daily-report access, a floating Ask Munim Ji button, easy notification access, touch-friendly buttons, expandable detail views.

5.19 "Every Card and Button Must Work"

No decorative dead UI, anywhere:

  • Business Card → Business Detail
  • Profit Card → Profit analysis
  • Critical Alert → Critical business details
  • Take Action → relevant business action
  • Ask Munim Ji → AI interface
  • Profile → Profile
  • Home → Dashboard
  • Notification → Notification popup
  • Download Report → Generate/download PDF
  • Chart filter → change chart mode
  • Manager actions → corresponding reporting/account workflow

6. Data Privacy Requirements (non-negotiable)

  • Business Owner: full access to their own business data only.
  • Manager: access strictly limited to their assigned business and reporting functions — no access to the owner's strategic dashboard.
  • Munim Ji AI: access only to the data required for its analysis, scoped to the authorized owner.
  • Platform operator: must not have unrestricted access to individual customers' business data by default. Prefer aggregated/anonymized platform analytics; any support access to identifiable data must be controlled, authorized, and logged.

This must be enforced at the database layer via Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) — not only in application code — so that tenant isolation holds regardless of what the frontend does.

Required checkpoint: Before any manager account with scoped access goes live (end of Phase 3, see Section 9), commission a paid review of the schema and RLS policies from an experienced Postgres/Supabase developer. Deliverable: a written report confirming two test businesses with two test managers cannot see each other's data under any tested query path, including direct API calls (not just the UI).

7. System Architecture

          Manager reporting tool                      Owner dashboard
          (daily health reports)                (recovery, break-even, alerts)
                    │                                       ▲
                    ▼                                       │
          ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │                        Supabase                         │
          │         Postgres + Auth + RLS + Realtime                │
          └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                    │                                       ▲
                    ▼                                       │
                        Munim Ji AI
                 (Edge Function + LLM verdicts)
                    │              │              │
                    ▼              ▼              ▼
             Razorpay ₹499   WhatsApp/Email    PDF reports
             (consultation)  (booking/alerts)  (watermarked)
  • Manager reporting tool and Owner dashboard are the two product-facing surfaces, both reading/writing through Supabase with RLS enforcing who can see what.
  • Supabase is the single source of truth: Postgres for data, Auth for both owner and manager identities (same system, different RLS-scoped permissions), Realtime so dashboards update without a manual refresh, Edge Functions for server-side logic.
  • Munim Ji AI is a Supabase Edge Function that reads business_metrics, calls an LLM with a structured prompt, and writes back to ai_verdicts. It also triggers the three external integrations when the owner takes action: payment, WhatsApp/email, and report generation.

Scalability notes:

  • Treat business_metrics as a scheduled/materialized computation refreshed periodically — do not compute it live on every dashboard load.
  • Index business_id and manager_id on every table where they appear as foreign keys.
  • Scope Supabase Realtime subscriptions to a specific business_id, not globally, so clients aren't listening to every business on the platform.
  • Run a separate staging Supabase project for development/testing; production is only touched with tested changes.

10. Development Phases & Timeline

Roughly 16 weeks (4 months) at a solo-founder pace, in six phases. Only one phase requires paid outside help before launch.

Phase 1 — Foundation: Supabase + Auth (Weeks 1–2) Set up the Supabase project (Postgres, Auth, RLS, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions). Scaffold the app in Lovable. Build profiles, owner signup/login, and the English/Hindi toggle skeleton early since it touches every screen. Solo build, no outside help needed.

Phase 2 — Business & Investment Layer (Weeks 3–4) Build businesses (including business_start_type), business_investments, business_baseline_snapshots, and the New-vs-Existing onboarding fork. Solo build.

Phase 3 — Manager Side: Daily Reporting (Weeks 5–7) Build business_managers, manager_assignments, daily_reports. Manager login scoped via RLS. Build the report submission form and the reporting calendar. → At the end of this phase, before any real manager account goes live: commission the one-time RLS security audit (~₹8,000–15,000, a freelance Supabase/Postgres developer, roughly a day of their time — see Section 6).

Phase 4 — Owner Dashboard & Business Detail (Weeks 8–10) Build business_metrics, the single switchable bar chart, consistent per-business colors, the five-tab Business Detail page, and scoped Realtime. By now there's a demoable product even without AI — a good moment to get real reactions from 2–3 trusted business owners, ideally including at least one running an existing (not brand-new) business.

Phase 5 — Munim Ji AI Layer (Weeks 11–13) Build ai_verdicts and the Edge Function (status, reason, financial impact, recommendation, priority, confidence), including the required Learning Period state. Build Ask Munim Ji as text-first. Wire notifications and the alert bell. Do not start this phase until Phase 3's daily reports are actually flowing — verdicts need real metrics to test against.

Phase 6 — Monetization, Reports & Polish (Weeks 14–16) Build consultancy_requests + Razorpay, WhatsApp/email/callback options, generated_reports with the watermark baked in, the Hindi translation pass, definition popups, mobile bottom nav, and a full QA pass confirming every card and button leads somewhere real.

11. Acceptance Criteria

  • A manager logged into Business A cannot retrieve any data belonging to Business B, verified by direct API testing, not just UI testing.
  • Every dashboard card, alert, and button navigates to a working, non-decorative destination.
  • The language toggle changes all UI text and AI/report content, but never numeric values.
  • PDF reports render with the watermark embedded in the file itself, not as a UI-only overlay.
  • Dashboard and Realtime updates function without requiring a manual page refresh.
  • An Existing business that marks investment as already-recovered never shows a recovery-progress metric on its dashboard.
  • A business with fewer than ~2–4 weeks of daily reports shows the AI's calibration/learning message, never a confident Growing/Stable-Watch/Critical verdict.
